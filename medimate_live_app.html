I've had a dull headache since this morning after working on my laptop for 4 hours. What could be causing this?

Headaches that develop after prolonged screen time are very frequently linked to eye strain, muscle tension in the neck, or mild dehydration.

Gentle comfort measures:
• Practice the 20-20-20 rule (look 20 feet away for 20 seconds).
• Sip a tall glass of room-temperature water.
• Rest in a quiet, dimly lit space for 15 minutes.

Note: If a headache comes on suddenly like a "thunderclap", consult a doctor immediately.
